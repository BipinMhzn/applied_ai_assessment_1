\section{Conclusion}

StackLearn is a working end-to-end product built on our own collection of 528,495 Stack Overflow
questions. Pre-trained MiniLM embeddings beat a tuned TF-IDF baseline on every metric while using a
seventh of the memory. Testing on, and fine-tuning with, real duplicate pairs is the clearest next step.
